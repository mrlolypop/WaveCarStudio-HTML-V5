On-location acoustic calibration at your residence, estate or private garage across Delhi NCR and beyond. Collaborative, unhurried, and tailored entirely to how you listen.

The doorstep protocol

Four steps. No rigid packages.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic audit

    We measure your system as it is with a calibrated microphone and RTA, and listen with you to your own music.

  2. 02

    Listening consultation

    You tell us what you love and what bothers you. We agree a target sound — warm, neutral, punchy or reference.

  3. 03

    DSP calibration

    Time alignment, EQ, crossovers and phase are tuned live in the car, measuring after every change.

  4. 04

    Final listening & handover

    We play back your tracks, fine-tune by ear, and save your new profile. You hear the before and after.
