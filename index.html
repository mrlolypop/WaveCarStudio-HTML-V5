What we do

Your car has a premium system. The cabin is ruining it.

Glass, leather and an off-centre seat turn even a Burmester or Bowers & Wilkins system into a lopsided experience: vocals stuck in the door, boomy bass, harsh cymbals, the stage leaning towards one ear.

WaveCarStudio is a specialist acoustic calibration studio. We measure your exact cabin with a real-time analyser, then programme the digital signal processor — time alignment, equalisation, crossovers and phase — so your music lands in front of you, exactly as recorded.

We don't sell hardware. We make the system you already own perform.
